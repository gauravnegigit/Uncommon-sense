Important Clinical Safety Notice Not a Diagnosis

GraminHealth is a triage and care referral assistant. It categorizes urgency (Emergency, Urgent, Routine) and directs patients to nearby facilities. It never attempts to diagnose diseases.

Instead of "What disease do I have?", We Answer 3 Real Questions

QUESTION 01

How urgently should I seek care?

मुझे कितनी जल्दी इलाज की आवश्यकता है?

Clinically reviewed rule engine classifies urgency: 🚨 Emergency (Immediate), ⚠️ Urgent (24-48 hrs), or 🟢 Routine (3-7 days).

QUESTION 02

Where should I go?

मुझे किस स्वास्थ्य केंद्र जाना चाहिए?

Recommends the appropriate public facility: Village Sub-Centre, 24x7 PHC, CHC, or District Hospital based on bed counts and distance.

QUESTION 03

What should I tell the doctor?

डॉक्टर को क्या जानकारी देनी चाहिए?

Generates a doctor-ready patient summary in both Hindi and English, ensuring smooth consultation readiness.
